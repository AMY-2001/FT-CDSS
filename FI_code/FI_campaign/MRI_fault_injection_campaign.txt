MRI FAULT-INJECTION CAMPAIGN
=============================

Dataset:
MRI brain-tumor dataset

Model:
ResNet18 (RN18)

Campaign scope:
This campaign records the effect of bit-flip (upset) fault injection on
classification accuracy for selected high-order (MSB) bit positions.
The campaign was reduced to the selected MSB positions because the
experimental analysis showed that higher-order bits produced more
significant accuracy degradation.

The values below are classification accuracy (%) for the corresponding
injection percentage and selected bit position.

INTERPRETATION
-----------------------------------------

The campaign focuses on upset/bit-flip faults at selected high-order
(MSB) bit positions. Three adjacent MSB regions were examined:

  • Bit 7 and associated multi-bit groups
  • Bit 6 and associated multi-bit groups
  • Bit 5 and associated multi-bit groups

The campaign was intentionally limited to these representative MSB
positions because the empirical results indicated greater sensitivity
to faults affecting higher-order bits. This provides a focused campaign
for documenting the fault-selection rationale rather than exhaustively
enumerating every possible bit position.
