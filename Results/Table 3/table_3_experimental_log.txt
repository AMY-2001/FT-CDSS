TABLE 3 — FAULT-TOLERANCE EVALUATION LOGS
_______________________________________________________________________

These logs contain the outputs used to generate Table 3.
All evaluations were performed on the complete test sets under the specified fault-injection conditions.

Definitions
-----------
TMR accuracy = (Correct TMR predictions / Total samples) × 100

DMR accuracy = ((Total agreements - False agreements) /
                Total samples) × 100

Fault masking coverage = (Post-fault accuracy / Pre-fault accuracy) × 100

SDC rate = (Incorrect undetected agreements / Total inferences under fault) × 100

An SDC is an incorrect final agreement/majority that is not detected by
the redundancy mechanism. For DMR, it is an incorrect agreement between
the two models. For TMR, it is an incorrect majority decision. For Hybrid,
it is an incorrect agreement in the initial DMR path that is not detected and does not invoke the cold spare.
