TABLE 4 — COMPUTATIONAL COST AND ENERGY EXPERIMENTAL LOG
_______________________________________________________________________


This log contains the measured and calculated values used to generate Table 4.

Energy calculation:
Energy (J) = Average power (W) × T_testing (s/image)

T_testing is the average inference time per image for the corresponding
model/redundancy configuration. It is distinct from the end-to-end pipeline
latency reported in Figures 5 and 6.

FLOPs calculation:
FLOPs = Total mult-add operations × 2.
DMR FLOPs = single-model FLOPs × 2.
TMR FLOPs = single-model FLOPs × 3.
Hybrid FLOPs = (A/N) × (FLOPs × 2) + (D/N) × (FLOPs × 3),
where A is the number of DMR agreements, D is the number of DMR
disagreements, N is the total number of samples, and FLOPs is the
single-model FLOPs.

Power measurement:
Average power was calculated from 10 power measurements for each
configuration using the KWS-066C USB Type-C USB Tester.
