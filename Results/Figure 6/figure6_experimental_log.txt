FIGURE 6 — WORKLOAD SCALABILITY / AVERAGE SPEEDUP LOG
_________________________________________________________________________

This log contains the sequential and parallel execution times used to generate Figure 6.

The configurations were evaluated using a combined workload of 2010 test images consisting of:

- 1000 colon cancer images
- 470 brain tumor MRI images
- 540 pneumonia X-ray images

Total: 2010 images

The evaluated workloads were:
1, 10, 100, 500, 1000, 1500, and 2010 images.

For each workload, the sequential and parallel execution times were recorded on the Raspberry Pi 4 Model B.

Average speedup is calculated using Eq. (7):

S_avg = (1/N) × Σ(T_seq,i / T_par,i)

where:
- N = 7 workload sizes
- T_seq,i = sequential execution time for workload i
- T_par,i = parallel execution time for workload i

The average speedup reported in Figure 6 is the arithmetic mean
of the seven individual speedups.
